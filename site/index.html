About

CSO Consulting Team is led by Stefan G. Graefe, a senior fundraising and strategy consultant with more than four decades of experience in international fundraising, organisational development and stakeholder communication.

We work with Civil Society Organisations that need practical, credible and sustainable approaches to resource mobilisation, major-gift fundraising, legacy giving, campaign development and strategic communication.

Our approach is direct, collaborative and implementation-oriented. We help organisations translate strategic ambitions into clear priorities, compelling cases for support and communication that resonates with donors, boards, partners and the people they serve.

For larger or more specialised assignments, we assemble a tailored consulting team from a trusted network of senior professionals. This allows us to combine deep sector experience with the right expertise for each specific challenge.

What “CSO” means

CSO stands for Civil Society Organisation: non-profit organisations, foundations, associations, social enterprises and other mission-driven organisations working for social impact.

What We Offer

  • Fundraising Strategy

    We develop realistic, long-term fundraising strategies aligned with an organisation’s mission, capacity and funding environment.

  • Major Gifts & Legacy Giving

    We design donor-centred approaches for major donors, foundations, legacy giving and long-term philanthropic partnerships.

  • Campaigns & Case for Support

    We create compelling fundraising cases, campaign concepts and donor communication for national and international audiences.

    We bring experience from Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe and North America to organisations working across borders and cultures.

Experience

Over the past four decades, we have worked closely with international civil society organisations, fundraising offices, boards and philanthropic partners.

A significant part of our work has been dedicated to Nuestros Pequeños Hermanos (NPH), an international organisation supporting children, young people and families through family-based care and community-strengthening approaches in Latin America and the Caribbean.

Stefan Graefe has supported NPH across multiple countries and regions, including fundraising strategy, European donor engagement, organisational development, campaign work and project-related advisory. This long-standing involvement provides a deep understanding of the realities facing international CSOs: complex stakeholder structures, evolving funding environments, local implementation challenges and the need to communicate mission and impact credibly across cultures.

Voluntary Commitment

Stefan Graefe serves as a voluntary Trustee of the Padre Wasson Foundation, supporting its mission and governance. This role reflects a long-term personal commitment to the values and communities at the heart of the NPH movement.
