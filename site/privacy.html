3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., USA (“Netlify”). Whenever you open a page, your browser automatically transmits certain information, which Netlify processes in server log files:

  • IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the website from which you came (referrer)
  • browser type and version, and operating system

Purpose: to deliver the website to your device and to keep it secure and stable, for example to detect and prevent attacks.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and efficient provision of this website.

Processor: Netlify processes this data on our behalf on the basis of a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).

Transfer to the USA: Netlify may process the data in the USA. Netlify participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, Netlify’s data processing agreement includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

Storage period: the log data is only kept for as long as it is needed for the purposes above. For details, see Netlify’s privacy policy: www.netlify.com/privacy.

4. Encrypted connection

This website uses TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the “https://” at the beginning of the address in your browser. It prevents third parties from reading or changing the data transmitted between your browser and this website.

5. Contact by e-mail or phone

If you contact us by e-mail or phone, we process the data you provide, such as your name, e-mail address, phone number and the content of your message, in order to deal with your enquiry.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or to steps taken before entering into a contract. In all other cases, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is responding to enquiries addressed to us.

E-mail provider: our e-mails are handled by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany, which processes them on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

Storage period: we delete your data once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations, for example under commercial or tax law, require us to keep it for longer.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set any cookies and does not access information stored on your device. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

7. Your rights

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to:

  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, simply contact us at lead@stefangraefe.org.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where we process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
